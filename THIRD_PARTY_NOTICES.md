## Qt and PySide6 (LGPL v3)

The user interface uses Qt through PySide6 (Qt for Python), licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public
Licence v3 (LGPL-3.0). The Windows build is a PyInstaller **one-folder** build: the Qt and PySide6 libraries are
separate, dynamically linked DLL and .pyd files in the `_internal` folder, not compiled into the program. You may
replace them with your own compatible build of Qt/PySide6 (same major version) by swapping those files, and you
can rebuild the whole application from this repository (`packaging/build.ps1`). The Qt source code is available
from https://download.qt.io/ and the PySide6 source from https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/.
The LGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 texts are included with PySide6 in the build.

## Bundled data

* Pickles (1998) stellar flux library (`starextractor/reference_spectra/`), see the credits above and
  `README.HILIB` in that folder.
* Reference-star list (`starextractor/data/reference_stars.csv`, provenance in `reference_stars.meta.json`): our
  own selection, generated by starex_refcat from SIMBAD, VizieR (the AAVSO VSX and the Washington Double Star
  Catalog) and the Pecaut and Mamajek (2013) intrinsic colours; see Help › Where the reference stars come from.
* AAVSO telluric line list (`starextractor/data/aavso_telluric_lines.csv`), from the AAVSO Spectroscopy
  Database Users Manual v1.4, appendix A.1.
* Kitt Peak atmospheric extinction (`starextractor/data/kpnoextinct.dat`), from IRAF `noao/lib/onedstds`.
* ZWO ASI585MM Pro gain/read-noise table (`starextractor/data/asi585mm_pro.csv`), from ZWO's published
  specifications.

## Data sources of the reference-star list

This research has made use of the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France, and of the VizieR
catalogue access tool, CDS, Strasbourg, France (DOI: 10.26093/cds/vizier). This research has made use of the
Washington Double Star Catalog maintained at the U.S. Naval Observatory.

* SIMBAD: Wenger, M., Ochsenbein, F., Egret, D., et al. 2000, "The SIMBAD astronomical database", A&AS 143, 9
  (2000A&AS..143....9W).
* VizieR: Ochsenbein, F., Bauer, P., Marcout, J. 2000, "The VizieR database of astronomical catalogues",
  A&AS 143, 23 (2000A&AS..143...23O).
* AAVSO International Variable Star Index (VSX): Watson, C. L., et al. 2006, Society for Astronomical Sciences
  25th Annual Symposium, 47 (2006SASS...25...47W); VizieR catalogue B/vsx.
* Washington Double Star Catalog: Mason, B. D., Wycoff, G. L., Hartkopf, W. I., Douglass, G. G., Worley, C. E.
  2001, "The 2001 US Naval Observatory Double Star CD-ROM. I. The Washington Double Star Catalog", AJ 122, 3466
  (2001AJ....122.3466M); VizieR catalogue B/wds.
* Intrinsic colours: Pecaut, M. J., Mamajek, E. E. 2013, "Intrinsic Colors, Temperatures, and Bolometric
  Corrections of Pre-main-sequence Stars", ApJS 208, 9 (2013ApJS..208....9P), Table 5; VizieR J/ApJS/208/9.
