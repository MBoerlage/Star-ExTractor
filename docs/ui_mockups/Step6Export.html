Roll the wheel over the wavelength axis to zoom X, or drag the amber window in the whole-spectrum strip. Dashed amber lines mark the Balmer lines at their rest wavelengths. Blue bands, dotted bandheads and baseline ticks are the telluric lines AAVSO uses to check calibration (H₂O near Hα, O₂ B at 687 nm, O₂ A at 759 nm). Quality check: the dips in your spectrum should sit on the blue ticks. If they are offset, check the wavelength calibration. Telluric features do not move with the star and stay in the published file.
