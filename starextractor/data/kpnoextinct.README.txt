kpnoextinct.dat — Kitt Peak standard atmospheric extinction, wavelength (Angstrom) and k (mag per airmass).
Source: IRAF noao/lib/onedstds/kpnoextinct.dat,
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iraf-community/iraf/main/noao/lib/onedstds/kpnoextinct.dat
